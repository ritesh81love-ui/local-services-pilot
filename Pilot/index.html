How Bundlup could work

Local services that work
for both sides.

For gardening, pest care, aircon servicing and other everyday jobs, people in the same or nearby suburbs could express interest in a local provider's offer. The provider could combine suitable visits into one area; everyone would know the offer and agree on a workable time.

01 / LOCAL OFFER
▣

See what is available nearby

A local provider could list a service with an individual option, a group option, or both, with clear terms.

02 / YOUR CHOICE
◉

Go solo or join nearby

Choose an individual visit if offered, or join friends and neighbours in your suburb or close by for a group offer.

03 / AGREED VISIT
↗

Find a time that works

Customers and provider agree on a practical appointment time. Nearby jobs could save travel and make the service worthwhile for both.
